# VCam 0.2.3 — English and Russian

The Windows receiver now supports English and Russian throughout the camera UI, settings, connection instructions, pairing errors and update messages. First launch uses Russian for a Russian Windows locale and English for other locales. You can change it in Settings → Language; the choice persists across restarts. Switching language does not stop an active camera session.

The installer offers English and Russian. English QR links open the English phone connection page. No changes to the native video transport or virtual camera identifiers.

The website has separate Russian and English pages, four English setup guides, requirements and direct download pages, crawlable FAQ answers, reciprocal language links, structured product information and an updated sitemap. The daily download counter keeps its last verified value during outages.

All implemented features remain free, without ads, a watermark or a subscription. Windows 10/11 x64; video only, same local Wi-Fi required. The beta installer remains unsigned with a publisher certificate.

Русский: интерфейс Windows, сообщения, инструкции и установщик доступны на двух языках. Выбор языка сохраняется; английский QR открывает английскую страницу подключения. Требования и ограничения камеры остаются прежними.
